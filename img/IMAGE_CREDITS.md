# Image sources and reuse

The seven photographs in `gallery/` were selected via [Pixabay image search](https://pixabay.com/images/search/) for the built-in quizzes on 2026-08-31. They are integrated into quiz questions, answers, covers and results, not offered as a stock-image collection or for standalone resale.

Each linked photo page identifies the image as available under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) ([full terms](https://pixabay.com/service/terms/)). That license permits free use and adaptation subject to restrictions, including no standalone distribution/resale. It is not a public-domain declaration. No sponsored iStock content, identifiable people, or recognizable commercial logos were selected.

Downloaded JPEG renditions have a maximum side of 1280 pixels. CSS crops them consistently without stretching; files are served by GitHub Pages rather than hotlinked. Repeated use across quizzes is intentional; no duplicate image files are stored.

`favicon.svg` and `fallback.svg` are project-created typographic UI assets. The original demo GIF, editing icons and panda image remain in the repository to preserve project history; the v2 build excludes these legacy assets. External community images are supplied by their quiz authors, are not copied to this repository, and cannot be independently licensed or moderated by this static app. Broken or unsafe image URLs show a local placeholder.
